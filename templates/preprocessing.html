Time-Series Integrity Protocol
Zero data leakage across forecasting horizons
Chronological Split
  • Chronological Partition: 2023–2024 forms the training baseline across 5 zones; 2025 serves as unseen forward evaluation.
  • Zone-Isolated Shifts: Rolling calculations are grouped strictly by Zone_ID so consumption in Zone Z01 never spills into Zone Z02.
  • Strict Lookahead Prevention: Day $t$ utilizes strictly $t-1$ and historical observations. Day $t$ target never leaks into predictors.
