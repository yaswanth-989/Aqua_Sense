Imputation & Leakage Prevention Architecture
Train-only parameter fitting protocol
Verified Safe

To strictly prevent data leakage from the test distribution into model training, all statistical imputers and scalers are fitted exclusively on the training partition:

  • Stratification: Target distribution (61.0% vs 39.0%) preserved identically in train and test splits.
  • Median Imputation: Median values are calculated on X_train and applied downstream to both splits.
  • Standardization: Feature centering and variance scaling fitted on train_imputed only.
  • Serialization: Artifact saved to models/potability/potability_preprocessor.joblib for production inference.

Chronological Split & Time-Series Integrity
Zero data leakage across forecasting horizons
Chronological Split

Unlike standard cross-sectional models, demand forecasting must never randomly shuffle rows. Time-aware rules applied:

  • Chronological Cutoff: Observations before 2025-01-01 form the train set (2 full years across 5 zones); year 2025 serves as strictly forward evaluation.
  • Zone-Partitioned Shifting: Lags and rolling windows are calculated strictly within each Zone_ID to prevent cross-zone signal mixing.
  • Warmup Pruning: Exactly 70 warmup observations (14 days × 5 zones) dropped from initial history so all lag inputs are fully populated.
