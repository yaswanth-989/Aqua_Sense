Task B — Municipal Water Demand Forecasting (Regression)

Time-Series · Chronological 80/20 Split

Predicts next-day water consumption in kiloliters (Water_Demand_kL) across 5 distribution zones. Lag and rolling-window features are engineered with zero future lookahead to prevent leakage. The entire year 2025 is held out as an unseen evaluation horizon.
